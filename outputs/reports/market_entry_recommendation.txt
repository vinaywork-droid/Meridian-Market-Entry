MERIDIAN CONSUMER GOODS PVT. LTD.
MARKET ENTRY RECOMMENDATION REPORT
Prepared by: Vinay Wakadkar
============================================================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Our market entry analysis culminates in a strong recommendation for Meridian Consumer Goods Pvt. Ltd. to enter the Nagpur market. With a weighted score of 0.6218, Nagpur emerges as the top-ranked city, driven by its high market attractiveness score of 0.8 and operational feasibility score of 0.9062. Our financial projections indicate that Nagpur offers the highest revenue potential, with a base scenario annual revenue of ₹12.84 crore, well within the client's budget of ₹8-12 crore for Year 1. We believe that Nagpur presents a compelling opportunity for Meridian to establish a strong foothold in the Tier 2 city market.

2. CITY ANALYSIS
Nagpur is characterized by its high market attractiveness, with a score of 0.8, driven by its large consumer base and growing disposable incomes. The competitive landscape in Nagpur is relatively weak, with a score of 0.0, indicating a significant opportunity for Meridian to establish a strong presence. Operationally, Nagpur is feasible, with a score of 0.9062, driven by its well-developed infrastructure and logistics network. Financially, Nagpur offers the highest revenue potential, with a base scenario annual revenue of ₹12.84 crore. However, it is essential to note that the conservative scenario projects a lower annual revenue of ₹6.81 crore, highlighting the importance of effective execution and risk mitigation.

In contrast, Nashik presents a more modest opportunity, with a weighted score of 0.4355. While its market attractiveness score is lower at 0.1764, Nashik's competitive landscape is more favorable, with a score of 0.4985. Operationally, Nashik is relatively less feasible, with a score of 0.6757. The financial projections for Nashik indicate a base scenario annual revenue of ₹5.98 crore, which is lower than Nagpur's but still within the client's budget. However, the conservative scenario projects a significantly lower annual revenue of ₹3.17 crore, emphasizing the need for careful planning and execution.

Mysuru, with a weighted score of 0.3730, is the least attractive option among the three cities. Its market attractiveness score is 0.2957, and the competitive landscape is highly competitive, with a score of 0.9963. Operationally, Mysuru is the least feasible, with a score of 0.0. The financial projections for Mysuru indicate a base scenario annual revenue of ₹3.96 crore, which is significantly lower than the other two cities. Furthermore, the breakeven analysis suggests that Mysuru would take 30 months to break even in the base scenario, making it a less viable option.

3. COMPARATIVE SCORECARD SUMMARY
The scorecard results clearly indicate that Nagpur is the top-ranked city, followed by Nashik and then Mysuru. Nagpur's high market attractiveness and operational feasibility scores drive its strong ranking. In contrast, Nashik's lower market attractiveness and operational feasibility scores result in a lower ranking. Mysuru's highly competitive landscape and low operational feasibility score make it the least attractive option. The weighted scores provide a comprehensive view of each city's overall attractiveness, taking into account multiple factors and their relative importance.

4. FINANCIAL PROJECTIONS SUMMARY
Our financial projections indicate that Nagpur offers the highest revenue potential, with a base scenario annual revenue of ₹12.84 crore. The conservative scenario projects a lower annual revenue of ₹6.81 crore, while the optimistic scenario projects a significantly higher annual revenue of ₹19.96 crore. In contrast, Nashik's base scenario annual revenue is ₹5.98 crore, with a conservative scenario of ₹3.17 crore and an optimistic scenario of ₹9.29 crore. Mysuru's financial projections are significantly lower, with a base scenario annual revenue of ₹3.96 crore. The breakeven analysis suggests that Nagpur would break even in 14 months in the base scenario, while Nashik would take 19 months and Mysuru would take 30 months.

5. RISK ASSESSMENT SUMMARY
The top three risks associated with entering the Nagpur market are competitive retaliation by HUL/P&G, distribution ramp-up being slower than benchmark, and consumer adoption being slower than benchmark. To mitigate these risks, we recommend securing distribution agreements before public launch, focusing on underpenetrated areas, and investing in product sampling campaigns. Additionally, hiring an experienced local distributor and setting distribution coverage milestones can help mitigate the risk of slower distribution ramp-up. Regular monitoring of sales performance and consumer adoption rates can also help identify potential issues early on.

6. FINAL RECOMMENDATION
Based on our comprehensive analysis, we strongly recommend that Meridian Consumer Goods Pvt. Ltd. enter the Nagpur market. With its high market attractiveness, relatively weak competitive landscape, and operational feasibility, Nagpur presents a compelling opportunity for Meridian to establish a strong foothold in the Tier 2 city market. We believe that the potential revenue of ₹12.84 crore in the base scenario, combined with the breakeven timeline of 14 months, makes Nagpur an attractive option. However, it is essential to carefully plan and execute the entry strategy, mitigating the associated risks to ensure successful market entry.

7. IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP
We propose a phased entry plan for Nagpur, with the following key milestones:
- Pre-launch: Secure distribution agreements, hire an experienced local distributor, and invest in product sampling campaigns.
- Month 1-3: Launch the product, focus on underpenetrated areas, and monitor sales performance and consumer adoption rates.
- Month 4-6: Review distribution coverage and adjust the strategy as needed, continue to invest in product sampling campaigns, and monitor consumer adoption rates.
- Month 7-12: Analyze sales data, adjust the marketing strategy, and plan for the next phase of expansion.
- Year 2 priorities: Continue to expand distribution coverage, invest in targeted marketing campaigns, and evaluate the feasibility of expanding into adjacent markets.
